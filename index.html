About Me

I'm a Ph.D. researcher in the Machine Intelligence Lab at the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley (UTRGV), with a passion for understanding how intelligent machines can learn, cooperate, and contribute to a better world. My research focuses on scalable and decentralized coordination in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL). I am particularly interested in developing methods that enable autonomous agents to cooperate effectively, make intelligent decisions using local information, and maintain efficient coordination as the number of agents grows.

My work also extends to vision-language agents and robot learning, with an emphasis on generalization across agents and environments and efficient adaptation to new challenges. Through my research, I aim to advance our understanding of how intelligent systems can address complex real-world problems. I believe the future of artificial intelligence lies not only in building systems that excel at individual tasks, but also in developing intelligent systems that can work together toward shared goals and make a meaningful impact on society.

Research

My work so far spans these areas:

Population Scaling in MARL

How do MARL algorithms behave as the number of agents grows? We designed and built MASSMARL, a GPU-native JAX simulation engine with one array-based API shared by 20 tasks and 10 MARL baselines. It simulates over 16 million agents on a single GPU, and its benchmarking tools identified where widely used baselines hit memory limits.

CoRL 2026 Workshop

Vision-Language Agents for Multi-Agent Control

We compared zero-shot and LoRA-adapted vision-language agents with IPPO and MAPPO in cooperative pursuit under visual and structural shifts. Vision-language models adapted from MARL demonstrations were more robust to unseen visual shifts, while the MARL baselines performed best on the training task.

SCALE Workshop at ICML 2026

Robot Learning and Embodied AI

For long-horizon robotic manipulation, we developed planner-routed LoRA specialists. For quadruped navigation, we pair a vision-language planner with a reinforcement learning controller for locomotion.

SCALE Workshop at ICML 2026 · ICMLA 2026

Reinforcement Learning for Real-World Decisions

We've also worked on causally guided exploration in sparse-reward environments, and on offline reinforcement learning for safe decision-making in personalized chemotherapy dosing and smart-grid control.

ACDSA 2025 · ICICT 2025
